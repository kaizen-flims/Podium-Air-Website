THE PERSON BEHIND PODIUM AIR

Prem Das.

Prem Das, known online as @nottt.premm and Kaizen, is a filmmaker, video editor and developer behind Podium Air.

Podium Air

Prem develops and maintains the Podium Air project under the GitHub account kaizen-flims. His work brings filmmaking, editing and software development together.

Podium Air is a downloadable music application with an official website, an Android release and desktop beta releases for Windows and macOS. It supports online listening, offline downloads and a local music library. It is a working application, not only a UI concept.

Podium Air builds on existing open-source work. Original project and contributor credits remain available in the application repository.
