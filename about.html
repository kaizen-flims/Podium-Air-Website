OFFICIAL APP INFORMATION

Podium Air.

Podium Air is a downloadable music application maintained and branded by Prem Das (Kaizen / @nottt.premm) under the GitHub account kaizen-flims.

Official website and source

The official Podium Air website is podiumair.pages.dev. Its application source and releases are in kaizen-flims/Podium-Air. The website has its own Podium-Air-Website repository.

Choose a Podium Air download or browse the official releases. The website introduces the app and provides download links; it is not itself a browser music player.

Supported downloads

The website lists an Android release for Android 8.0 or later, plus desktop beta downloads for Windows x64 and macOS on Apple Silicon or Intel Macs. Desktop beta installers are unsigned; macOS builds are also unnotarized. Check the release notes for each build.

What does the app do?

Podium Air brings online music, offline downloads and a local music library together. Its Android features include background playback, lyrics and playback controls. Available features can vary by build and platform; the release notes and source are the reference for a particular version.

Is Podium Air the same as Podium?

Names alone can be ambiguous. Prem's kaizen-flims/Podium is a separate offline Android music player with a click-wheel interface. Podium Air is the streaming and discovery project at kaizen-flims/Podium-Air.

The separately published GHOSTxASR/podium-music-player repository calls its app Podium. It is not the Podium Air repository or download location. Use the full app name and the official links above to identify Podium Air.

Who maintains Podium Air, and what is its origin?

Prem Das maintains and brands Podium Air. It is a modified fork of BitChord by Kushagra Singh and contributors. The upstream credits and original notices remain in the source repository. Podium Air is not affiliated with Google or YouTube.
